About

inkling is a personal assistant that lives in your WhatsApp. It knows your email, your calendar and the people you talk to, and keeps track of what matters to you. There's nothing new to learn: you text it the way you'd text a friend, on your own or in a group chat, and it texts back. When something needs doing on a website, it opens its own browser and does it the way you would. It picks up the messages you meant to reply to, reminds you before things slip, makes the plan, books the table and fills in the form, and it always checks with you before anything goes out in your name or costs you money.

inkling is invite only for now. You can join the waiting list here, or ask someone who already uses it to add you.
